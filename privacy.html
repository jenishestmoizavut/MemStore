MemStore Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Developer: Jenish (JenishDotOne)

MemStore is designed to be personal, private, and local-first: your one-good-thing-a-day reflections live directly on your own device. This policy explains with complete transparency how your data is handled, stored, backed up, and protected.

1. Open Source Philosophy

MemStore is open-source and distributed under the MIT License. You have complete transparency into how the software operates.

2. Local Storage by Default

Every daily entry, draft, timestamp, golden star, and archived journal is stored directly in your device's private internal storage. It works 100% offline with zero latency and requires no account. If you never sign in, nothing you write ever leaves your device.

3. Manual Backups (Plain Text Notice)

⚠️ Plain Text Notice: Exported JSON backups are saved in human-readable plain text (not encrypted) directly onto your device's file storage. This ensures you have 100% data ownership with zero vendor lock-in. Because these files are unencrypted, please store your exported backup files in a secure location.

4. Optional Cloud Backup (Google Sign-In & Firestore)

Cloud synchronization is entirely optional, but helps protect your memories against device loss, damage, or phone upgrades. If you choose to sign in:

  • Account Identifier: Your Google Account ID, email, and display name are used solely to authenticate your session and link your journal.
  • Client-Side End-to-End Encryption: Your memories are encrypted locally on your device (AES-256-GCM) before transmission to Google Cloud Firestore. The data residing on cloud servers is ciphertext — readable text never reaches our servers.
  • Sync Metadata: Minimal synchronization timestamps are stored to ensure your devices stay up to date.

5. Zero Third-Party Tracking or Selling

Your memories belong exclusively to you. We do not sell, rent, trade, or distribute your private reflections, personal identifiers, or app data to third-party data brokers, ad networks, or commercial entities.

6. Offline Daily Reminders

If daily notifications are enabled, reminders run entirely on-device using local system alarms. They do not contact external servers or track your notification engagement.

7. Data Retention & Control

Local data persists on your device until you clear app data, uninstall, or start a new journal. You can export a backup, delete entries, or sign out anytime. To request cloud data deletion, contact us directly.
